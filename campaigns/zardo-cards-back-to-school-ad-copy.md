## 2. Flags before launch

1. **No promo code is visible on any creative.** All copy below assumes the discount is **applied automatically at checkout**. If a code exists (e.g. `SCHOOL10`), swap the `[CODE]` placeholder in the marked lines.
2. **End-date mismatch.** Creative 1 says "for a limited time"; creatives 4 and 5 say "ends Monday August 31st". Copy below always states the hard date — it converts better and it's the safer claim. Consider re-exporting creative 1 to match.
3. **"Rated 4.9 out of 5"** should be paired with the source (e.g. "4.9/5 from X Google reviews") wherever platform policy requires substantiation. Meta may reject an unsourced rating claim in the primary text.
4. **"Ships within 24 hours"** — confirm this holds through the weekend before running it on a sale ending Monday.
5. Avoid implying endorsement by The Pokémon Company. Keep it "Pokémon cards at Zardo Cards", never "official Pokémon sale".
